Privacy Policy

This OAuth client is used solely by its owner, on the owner's own computer, to download files from Google Drive folders that are shared with the owner, using read-only access (drive.readonly).

No data is collected, stored on any server, sold, or shared with any third party. Downloaded files and access tokens stay only on the owner's personal computer.

隱私權政策

此 OAuth 用戶端僅供擁有者本人在自己的電腦上使用，以唯讀權限（drive.readonly）下載分享給本人的 Google 雲端硬碟檔案。

不會收集任何資料，不會存放在任何伺服器，也不會出售或提供給任何第三方。下載的檔案與授權憑證只保存在擁有者的個人電腦中。
